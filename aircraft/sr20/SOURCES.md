# SR20 G6 systems research and applicability

Research checked October 11, 2026. This document records the evidence behind the SR20 expansion and the differences that prevent a direct SR22T copy.

## Model configuration

The model represents an early factory Perspective+ **SR20 G6**, serial **2220 or 2339–2900**, with a **Lycoming IO-390-C3B6**, standard three-blade metal propeller, MD302 standby, and 2+1 rear seat. The geometry reference remains OO-CBB, serial 2347. Optional air conditioning is represented; the Avionics panel can fit/remove the optional second GSU 75 ADAHRS and associated magnetometer/OAT connections. Avionics options shown in the catalogue are identified as optional in their notes.

The SR22T model provided the component-depth and interaction benchmark. Its Continental six-cylinder engine, turbochargers, intercoolers, wastegate, pressurized magnetos, TKS and oxygen systems are not SR20 equipment. Likewise, older Continental IO-360 SR20 fuel/start procedures, early vacuum/Lamar electrical systems, and later serial 8500-and-up equipment do not define this model.

The engine detail was also checked against three **manufacturer documents actually downloaded and read**:

- [Lycoming IO-390-C Installation and Operation Manual, January 2017](https://www.lycoming.com/sites/default/files/attachments/IOM-IO-390-C%2520Series%2520-%25201-31-17.pdf): System Description pp. 1–6, especially the C3B6 front governor, cylinder numbering/firing order, starter, injection and oil diagrams; installation table p. 14; Appendix A p. 51.
- [Lycoming IO-390-C Maintenance Manual, January 2017](https://www.lycoming.com/sites/default/files/attachments/MM-IO-390-C%2520Series%2520-%25201-31-2017.pdf): Introduction p. xx Figure 4; 72-25 pp. 174–175 (accessory-housing oil pump), 72-50 pp. 221–222 (pressure relief and filter-base thermostat), 72-70 pp. 237–238 (starter/ring gear).
- [Lycoming IO-390-C Illustrated Parts Catalog, Revision 1, June 2025](https://www.lycoming.com/sites/default/files/file/2025-06/PC-IO-390-C%20Series%20Rev%201%20%281%29.pdf): 72-20 Figure 7 pp. 16–17 and 72-60 Figure 24 pp. 58–59; C3B6 applicability columns. The later parts catalogue verifies component roles and does not override Cirrus operating limits.

These checks locate the thermostatic oil bypass at the filter base and the pump inside the accessory housing. The generic Lycoming installation table gives different thermostat/pressure figures from the aircraft POH; the study retains the POH’s 170 °F/18 psi description without simulating a pressure or thermal response. POH 7-40 and AMM 71-00 also differ in their cooling-baffle material wording; the baffle notes disclose that difference. AMM 61-00 describes a lower-power governor target near 1,900 RPM, while the POH describes the 2,500-to-2,700 schedule used by the existing simulator.

Additional electrical disagreements: POH 7-91/Figure 7-11 specifies a 5 A AVIONICS FAN 1 circuit whereas AMM 21-20 describes 3 A; the model retains the POH rating. AMM 32-42 describes brake-temperature sensor supply from MAIN BUS 2 through ENGINE INSTR, while the engine-airframe unit’s modeled ENGINE INSTR feed follows the POH’s ESS BUS 2. The sensor supply and indicated-data availability are kept distinct and labeled schematic.

## Approximation boundaries

- Component identities and connections are sourced; dimensions, small-part stations, tube diameters and bend paths are schematic unless a component note identifies a verified value.
- Internal meshes and complete curved tube walls are checked against the modeled fuselage, wing and tail skins. Only short transitions at named exterior ports are allowed to penetrate them. This verifies the drawing's physical consistency, not measured installation clearances.
- Preflight tip, leading-edge, wing/fuselage junction and cowling highlights use narrow skin overlays. SR20 stall strips follow AMM 57-20 p. 1 (inboard leading edges of both wing cuffs); their station, length and section remain schematic. The highlight overlay boundaries are display choices. Pitot-heat verification points to the probe while retaining the cockpit switch highlight.
- Wire particles explain signal/power availability; pitot/static particles explain pressure paths. Neither is a circuit-current calculation or fluid-dynamics simulation.
- Engine starting/starvation delays, cooling/oil-flow rates, brake motion, CAPS timing and electrical currents/endurance remain illustrative. Battery endurance uses the pre-existing training-deck rule of thumb, not a dispatch guarantee.
- The primary display keeps the existing fixed cruise picture. ADAHRS power/failure, screen reversion and breaker behavior are interactive; alignment, comparison logic, AFCS flight dynamics, fuel totalizer reconciliation and thermodynamic engine monitoring are not fully simulated.
- The public AMM text omits some drawings/chapters. Unsupported detail is labeled approximate or omitted rather than filled with SR22T hardware. Use the current serial-specific POH, approved supplements, airworthiness limitations and maintenance instructions for the airplane.
